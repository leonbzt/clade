# Playtest 08: lineages (27 Oct)

Your notes from chat, condensed.

**Liked**
- The new tree of life looks really cool; you like the idea a lot. The menu and tool bars too.
- Hunting for new variants was a lot of fun. Founding still feels cool.
- The gene icon look per variant.
- Larger parts of the map are colonisable now.

**The big issue: how species and clades flow into each other**
- The Leafy Liverwort that grew the right adaptations should *become* the moss species, not stay a liverwort while
  a separate Mosses species is founded from the data.
- Founding a variant as a new species leaves two near-identical populations: the old variant in the parent and the
  new species. The new species should start *as* that variant, or similar.
- A new clade discovered from one of your variants would be very exciting.

**Unclear**
- Which variant to pick as a new species feels random. You don't see why a variant becomes popular, or why one
  pick is better than another.
- It still feels a bit like filling the mutation bar until it's full. That can be good, but how it fills isn't visible.
- You didn't see the precursor checklist (it's only under an open node in the tree screen).
- The gene icons: hard to tell which is which. Idea: two arms (negative and positive), or one section per gene
  filled green for + and red for −, with a few bars each.

**Map**
- Really patchy now: many variants that don't blend together, and the bigger habitable area adds to it.
- Still too many sprites when zoomed in.
