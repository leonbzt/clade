# Patch notes

Newest first, written for players. *AI draft (5 Nov), yours to change.*

## Reading a habitat (5 Nov 2026)

- Each habitat card shows what its roles live on: mats, buried food, prey and oxygen, each marked against what's normal there.
- The card tells you what your next board holds before you start.
- A new font, with a real bold.
- Map labels no longer run off the edge of the map.

## The first playable sea (M1 run, 5 Nov 2026)

Clade was a plant game; it is now a Cambrian sea you live in, one creature at a time.

**Fixed after the first look**
- A form you keep is now really on your body. It takes its slot first, and the loadout shows when a verb has no room.
- You can leave your form behind for a Life, for example to get Dig back.
- A finished Life is saved the moment it ends. Close the page and its clutch is waiting when you come back.
- A rare part you laid after getting is now really passed on: it's a sure, face-up card in your clutch.
- You no longer have to take a new form from a clutch. Stay with the one you have, or keep none.
- Clutch cards show which slot a part takes.
- The Feel dev button is gone from player builds (the ` key still opens the panel).

**New: three habitats around a volcanic island**
- Sand shallows (low tide), mud flats and volcanic rock, each with its own ground, food and dangers.
- Each habitat has three niches: grazer, burrower and hunter. Species are ranked S to F by the eggs they lay.
- Habitat cards show open niches first, the rank to beat, who holds each row and the local news.

**New: lives**
- A Life is 20 turns. Walk, eat, grow up, moult new parts, and lay eggs before the season ends or something eats you.
- Three bodies: the worm, the arthropod and the swimmer.
- The niche you pick sets your body. Grazers live as arthropods, burrowers as worms, hunters as swimmers.
- What you ate decides which niche you filled.

**New: verbs**
- Pick 1 to 3 of six verbs before a Life. Each one grows a part, and parts cost upkeep.
- Three ready-made builds: sweeper, digger and tank.
- Mats now regrow after 4 turns.

**New: the hunter's Life**
- Prey act by their own rules: worms burrow, trilobites run, Wiwaxia hunkers down behind its spines.
- Lurk for a turn and prey won't notice you, and your next swim goes twice as far.
- Frenzy adds speed for each catch in a row.
- Compound eyes preview where prey will go.

**New: clutches and forms**
- After a Life, your eggs hatch as common, rare and epic cards. Keep one as your form in that niche.

**New: the living garden**
- Your eggs join the garden, which keeps running while you're away: one generation every 3 minutes, up to 8 hours offline.
- The next board you walk on is written from what the garden became.
- Natives you beat adapt and can take their row back.
- Lead a hunter row and your own hunters start chasing your grazers and burrowers there.
- Reach an A tier as an arthropod to unlock the trilobite.

**New: saves and sharing**
- Saves are versioned and keep a backup. You can copy your save from the Menu.
- Share a species code; a friend's species visits your garden as a rival to beat.
- The whole game now builds to one HTML file (764 KB) for itch.

**Changed**
- The board now runs on PixiJS, chosen in a head-to-head test against Godot.
- New menus in a stone-and-brass frame, textured ground, and the arthropod is now drawn.

**Known issues**
- Closing the page during a Life still loses that Life; only a finished Life is saved.
- The pixel font makes some digits and letters hard to tell apart (5 and S, 8 and B).
- Your species barely changes the boards yet, and the island map doesn't move.
