# Carapace (turtle evolution game): research, 9 Nov 2026

## Summary
- A pre-release solo indie game: "an Indie turtle evolution game" (Instagram bio). No store page, demo or build found.
  Steam is promised to Patreon backers "at launch". Small: 937 Instagram followers, 48 YouTube subscribers, about 1.2–2.8k views per short.
- Everything we know comes from 11 YouTube shorts (9 with a voice-over, read from the auto-captions) and 5 Instagram
  reels. These are marketing claims, so nothing here says whether it plays well.
- The pitch is close to Clade's Life loop: "when you die, you become one of your own hatchlings, and your body changes
  with what you do". Over the generations your family becomes a new species.
- It started as a fan game of Ollie Titley's spec-evo world *Kappa: World of Turtles* ("every vertebrate is a turtle").
  The dev asks fans to tag Titley, and a later reel says "I had to abandon the concept of kappa".
- Look: low-poly 3D drawn with a pixel shader, diorama tiles, a chunky pixel UI. "My turtle game has 0 image files":
  everything is drawn in code.

## 1. Core loop (voice-over, short 1UEtyDDxqJI)
- You die, then you play one of your own hatchlings. Use shapes the body: "Swim a lot, you grow flippers. Crack shells,
  rock crusher jaws. Live in the dark, giant eyes that glow."
- A generation card (frame at 0:13): a line with a silhouette, then "Gen 2", a Latin binomial ("…chelys macroura"),
  "+22 years", "31 days", and three trait icons. The next card scrolls in from the right.
- A "NEW SPECIES!" banner over the new form in the world (0:16).

## 2. World and sim (shorts GR2RKvwRIyA, 5MQoUqUUiu8)
- Five seasons, each with its own rules: wet bloom (marshes flower, rivers rise), warm dry (ponds dry, turtles bury
  themselves and sleep), monsoon (floods), cool (fog), cold (ice, sleep until the bloom).
- A food web: grazers (soft leaves, tough greens); hunters (snails, crayfish, eggs, turtles); scavengers (carrion,
  some crush bones).
- Events: insects swarm at night, green mats choke still water, triops hatch in rain pools, and mussels "fish for
  turtles": bite the lure and it bursts into larvae (a real freshwater-mussel trick).

## 3. Verbs and social life (shorts 5ofhGW3hwSE, Hg-bzHf9_rA, WU3C-u1qYrE)
- Herds, flocks and loners. Hunters ambush from the mud, drop from above, or run you down. In a fight you bite back or
  get flipped onto your back.
- Courtship: you bob to the beat, and if you miss the partner walks away. Named individuals (rival, neighbour).
- Verbs: swim, dig a nest, dance, dig up fossils, hide in mud, climb a rock tower and jump off it.

## 4. Places and content (shorts RDAhdbvjp0g, 7_bqsHEm21M, HVFu-3QqNzA, eLmUjYud3ew)
- Nine hidden "wonders". Two of them steer evolution: near the Basalt Organ, displays and sails grow stronger; near
  the Lantern Grove (glowing mushrooms), glow spots grow faster. You can walk inside the ribs of the biggest turtle that ever lived.
- Patreon votes on new turtles, each one body plus one trick: Rollstone (locks up and rolls downhill), Emberback (stores
  heat, quick in the cold), Musk bomb (hunters lose its trail), Wayfinder (magnetic homing), Warshell (eats poison
  mushrooms, so it's toxic to bite).
- A mutation system (dwarfism, albinism) is "being built" after a fan comment asked for it.
- Type a name and get a turtle: "same name, same turtle every time". Backers get theirs as a profile picture and a credit.

## 5. Marketing pattern
- Shorts of 10–45 s: a hook line, about five quick concrete examples, then a question to the viewer ("which season would
  your turtle survive?"). Patreon votes decide content.
