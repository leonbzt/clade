# The real tree as a game map (Cambrian roster check + whole-tree stepping stones)

Researched 10 Nov 2026. Builds on `tree-of-life-overview.md`, `eras-and-tree.md`, `cambrian-world.md` (not repeated). Tags: **[V]** page read this session; **[S]** from a search-result summary (page not opened); **[B]** background knowledge, not re-verified. "Debated" = flagged where the literature disagrees.

## Summary
- The ladder's biggest problem: "Worm -> Bristle worm" crosses the Ecdysozoa/Spiralia split. Priapulid worms (Ottoia) are ecdysozoans, same clade as arthropods; bristle worms and Wiwaxia are spiralians; Pikaia is a deuterostome. Three separate branches from a worm-like bilaterian.
- Second: "Lobopod -> Opabinia -> Anomalocaris" treats sister groups as a chain. Opabiniids and radiodonts are sister stem-arthropod lines, and both sit *between* lobopods and true arthropods, not beside them.
- Third: "Arthropod -> Trilobite" is fine as a grade, but Sidneyia, trilobites, Marrella and "shore crawler" are on different twigs; placement of artiopods (trilobites, Sidneyia) is still debated.
- Fact 1 (lobopods near arthropod root): TRUE for gilled lobopods and siberiids; Hallucigenia/Aysheaia are further out, and placement is debated.
- Fact 2 (tidal-flat trackways): PARTLY TRUE. Protichnites -> euthycarcinoids (body fossils tie them). Climactichnites -> NOT euthycarcinoid; a slug-like animal, probably a mollusc. "First on land" is tentative; trace evidence only.
- Fact 3 (true crabs ~Jurassic): TRUE with a caveat. Earliest confirmed Brachyura are Early Jurassic (Pliensbachian); crabs stay rare until the Cretaceous "crab revolution"; Eocarcinus is disputed (stem-crab vs anomuran).
- Whole tree: ~8 trunk gates (photosynthesis, mitochondria/eukaryote, plastids, multicellularity, hard parts, eyes, jaws, land) and 4-5 trait types that evolved many times, so recipes should be defined by what a body can do, not by who has it.

### How to keep the ladder honest (all **idea**-grade, caller decides)
- Root: a "worm-like bilaterian" (an unnamed root node). The real split of Protostomia into Ecdysozoa and Spiralia, plus Deuterostomia, comes from it [V] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecdysozoa . A molecular clock puts Ecdysozoa at ~636-578 Ma, and an Ediacaran possible ecdysozoan (Uncus, 2024) exists, so all three lines predate the Cambrian.
- Three coloured branches: Ecdysozoa (priapulid worm -> lobopod -> radiodont/opabiniid grade -> arthropod -> trilobite / shore crawler), Spiralia (bristle worm, Wiwaxia, hyolith, brachiopod), Deuterostomia (Pikaia, early echinoderms). Chaetognaths marked "unplaced protostome".
- Grades vs clades: player-facing nodes are body-plan grades (worm, lobopod, arthropod: paraphyletic by nature). Each carries a small tag for its real clade. "Worm" can be a grade shared by three clades, so the game can offer one Worm start and have the first innovation pick the branch (gut/cuticle -> ecdysozoan; parapodia/chaetae -> annelid; notochord -> chordate).
- Say "stem-X" or "relative of X" in tooltips: Burgessochaeta = stem annelid, Hurdia = stem arthropod. Matches the real wording and avoids false descent claims.
- Radiodonts and opabiniids as one side branch off the lobopod-to-arthropod stem; display it as a fork, not Opabinia -> Anomalocaris.

## 2. Three facts

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Lobopods are near the arthropod root | **Mostly true.** Gilled lobopods (Kerygmachela, Pambdelurion) and siberiids are widely accepted stem-arthropods basal to radiodonts. "Lobopodia" itself is a paraphyletic grade. Hallucigenia/Aysheaia: debated, may be outside the arthropod stem | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobopodia , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiodonta , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallucigenia (Smith & Caron 2015 Nature 523:75, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14573 [S]) |
| Cambrian tidal-flat trackways -> euthycarcinoids ("first onto shore") | **Half true.** Protichnites: yes. Body fossils of euthycarcinoids (Mosineia, Mictomerus) found at Blackberry Hill, Wisconsin; Collette & Hagadorn 2010; Collette, Gass & Hagadorn 2012 (J Paleontol 86:442). Tracks in intertidal/supratidal sand, ~530-457 Ma range for the ichnogenus. Climactichnites: NO. A slug-like animal, most likely a mollusc (Getty & Hagadorn 2008, 2009). "Among the first animals on land" is tentative (MacNaughton 2002 "First steps on land" Geology: Ontario eolian sandstone). Euthycarcinoids may have come ashore to mate/lay eggs (stated as a hypothesis). Some tracks may be stranded animals. Other candidate makers (aglaspidids, eurypterids, horseshoe crabs) were proposed. Wikipedia gives Climactichnites as "~510 Ma, Middle to Late Cambrian", internally inconsistent: treat date as Late Cambrian-ish | [V] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protichnites , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climactichnites , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euthycarcinoidea ; [S] https://doi.org/10.1666/11-056.1 |
| True crabs (Brachyura) appear around the Jurassic | **True.** Earliest confirmed brachyurans are Early Jurassic (Pliensbachian), e.g. Eoprosopon (Bavaria; Haug & Haug 2014 put it in Homolodromioidea). Eocarcinus (Pliensbachian, UK) is debated: moved to Anomura by Feldmann & Schweitzer 2010, returned as stem-crab by Scholtz 2020. Crabs rare in Middle Jurassic; diversification in the Cretaceous "crab revolution". Guinot 2019: some enigmatic taxa may be true crabs (hypothesis) | [S] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eocarcinus , https://www.wikipedia.com/wiki/Cretaceous_crab_revolution , Treatise Online https://journals.ku.edu/treatiseonline/article/download/25213/22431/98104 . Carcinisation page lists Brachyura first appearance Early Jurassic [V] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carcinisation |

Game note: a "shore crawler" cliffhanger is real history if worded as "trackways in tidal sand, probably an euthycarcinoid"; do not use Climactichnites as the shore crawler.

## 4. Where accuracy matters to players vs where games can simplify
- **Must be right (biology-literate audience):** which branch a creature is on (no priapulid -> annelid; fungi not plants; insects inside Crustacea), the "tree not ladder" feel (real groups named in hindsight), dates as ranges. Mark debated nodes (Wiwaxia, hyoliths, euthycarcinoids, lobopod roots) as "disputed" in tooltips; that is itself content.
- **Safe to simplify:** grades as nodes, a single "worm" start, a compressed timeline, role names (grazer, sifter) as long as labelled as inference. Feeding in the Cambrian is mostly inferred (cambrian-world.md).
- **Spore:** Science (Oct 2008, Bohannon with biologists) said it "failed miserably" on evolution; no common ancestors so a linear path; player choice read as intelligent design; Wright called it a toy [S] https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/gdc-lessons-learned-from-i-spore-i-its-science-and-more , https://trevorowens.org/vitae/teaching-intelligent-design-or-sparking-interest-in-science-what-players-do-with-will-wrights-spore/ .
- **22-game study (Leith et al., doi 10.1007/s10956-016-9620-x):** only 5 of 22 modelled all three Darwinian principles (Creatures, SimEarth, SimLife, Selection Game, Who Wants to Live a Million Years). The commonest sin: the player saves unfit organisms, so fitness does not decide survival; second: change during one lifetime instead of inherited [V] https://theconversation.com/playing-a-science-based-video-game-it-might-be-all-wrong-59874 . Clade's loop (egg inheritance, then competition on a tier list) sits on the right side of both.
- **Thrive:** microbe stage complete; nucleus unlock to become eukaryote, then organelles (mitochondria, chloroplast) incl. endosymbiosis; designer prefers specialisation over direct enhancement [S] https://wiki.revolutionarygamesstudio.com/wiki/Microbe_Progression .
- **Cell to Singularity:** tech-tree style branches (main, mammals, human) on an incremental frame; found no scientific accuracy review [S] https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Cell_to_Singularity . Nothing found on Species: ALRE, The Sapling, Evolution board game.

## Ideas for Clade
- **idea**: One unnamed "worm-like bilaterian" root, and the first body-part pick (cuticle / parapodia / notochord) chooses Ecdysozoa, Spiralia or Deuterostomia: honest, and a real "build" choice.
- **idea**: Define recipes by function (eye, shell, flight, legs, multicellular), so the same recipe can unlock on several lineages, with a "convergent" badge when a second line gets it.
- **idea**: Show disputed placements (Wiwaxia, hyoliths, euthycarcinoids) as "discovered, rank uncertain" nodes that firm up as the player collects clues, turning the debate into discovery.
