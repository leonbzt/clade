# More standout species and new body plans for kits (research, 17 Nov)

Follows `standout-species-kits.md` (the first six). Three parts: the rest of PR 3's list, six new candidates, and
four body plans that would be whole new kits. **Ideas, not the plan:** nothing joins the roster here.

**Format:** parts by the kit's six places (front, head, limbs, sides, back, tail), up to 3 rungs, at most 2 traits,
a verb from today's six (graze, dash, dig, sense, brace, crush) or a flagged new one (budget 8).
**Grade:** ✓ = read today on the linked Wikipedia page; ~ = known biology, not on that page, check before a card.

## Summary
- **Best new finds:** the **man o' war and the blue dragon** (a drifter colony and the slug that eats it and steals
  its stings: a predator-prey pair for the board), the **blue-ringed octopus** (warning flash as a verb), the
  **Sally Lightfoot crab** (black on black lava as a young crab: the volcanic island itself), the **sea krait**
  (must come ashore: a land crosser that is not a fish).
- **From PR 3's rest:** the **cuttlefish** and **frogfish** are as strong as the first six; the **chiton** and
  **horseshoe crab** are great cards with thin verbs; the **cormorant** and **solar slug** work as one trait each,
  not as kits.
- **New body plans:** cephalopod, drifting cnidarian colony, sea star (radial) and air-breathing land arrival. Each
  needs one kit rule today's kit lacks (below), so they are W3 or later.
- **Correction to the first six:** mantis shrimp come as **smashers** and **spearers** ✓: two builds of the same
  front place, a clean either/or (`kit.ts`: two parts on one place). Fiddler crabs regrow a lost big claw on the
  **same** side, weaker ✓; the pistol shrimp's "claw swap" stays ~ until checked.

## Part 1: the rest of PR 3's fifteen

### Common cuttlefish, *Sepia officinalis* — strong ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_cuttlefish))
- **Front:** eight arms and two **feeding tentacles** shot from ambush ✓. Rungs: *arms* → *tentacle strike* →
  *ambush from sand*. Bite; verb dash (the strike) or a new verb **Lunge** ~.
- **Head:** two highly developed eyes ✓ (senses). **Sides:** skin of **chromatophores, leucophores, iridophores**,
  four camouflage patterns, and skin texture it can change ✓ (defence as hiding). **Back:** internal **cuttlebone** ✓
  (buoyancy; speed ~). **Tail:** fins ~; the **siphon** jet ✓ (dash).
- **Traits:** *ink* (several ink types, released when it jets away ✓: escape a strike once a beat); *buried by day*
  (it hunts at night from under the sand ✓: dig).
- **Card:** "It can change its camouflage before it hatches." ✓ (embryos move their chromatophores in the egg).

### Frogfish (sargassumfish, *Histrio histrio*) — strong ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frogfish))
- **Front:** a mouth that widens up to **12 times**; a strike in **6 ms**; swallows prey **twice its size** ✓. Bite.
- **Head:** the **illicium and esca**, a lure shaped like a fish, shrimp or worm, withdrawn when not in use and
  **regrown if lost** ✓. Rungs: *lure* → *prey-shaped lure* → *regrowing lure*. **New verb candidate: Lure** (food
  comes to you): the one verb in this doc that changes the walk itself.
- **Limbs:** walks on **pectoral and pelvic fins**; fins grip weed strands to climb ✓ (speed low). **Sides:**
  scaleless skin with spinules; colour change over days; some grow algae on themselves ✓ (defence as hiding).
  **Tail:** jet by forcing water through gill openings ✓ (dash).
- **Traits:** *wait* (stays still: thrift high, speed low ~); *drifter* (lives in floating sargassum ✓: a board
  habitat of its own).
- **Card:** "It swallows prey in six milliseconds." ✓

### Seahorse, *Hippocampus* — a brood kit, slow ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seahorse))
- **Front:** long snout that sucks prey in (**pivot feeding**) ✓; no stomach, eats almost all the time ✓ (low thrift).
- **Head:** eyes that move **independently** ✓ (senses). **Back:** bony **ring plates**, no ribs ✓ (defence).
  **Limbs:** dorsal fin flutter, pectoral fins steer ✓; the slowest fish, about 1.5 m an hour (dwarf) ✓ (speed 0).
  **Tail:** **prehensile tail** of square rings, wraps a holdfast ✓ (verb brace).
- **Traits:** *male pregnancy* (up to 1,500 eggs in the pouch, 9–45 days ✓: brood, and the clutch survives better);
  *dawn courtship* (a dance that can last about 8 hours ✓).
- **Card:** "The father gives birth." ✓
- **Verdict:** the clearest "brood" build in the sea; too slow to be fun alone, good as a second clade on a line.

### Chiton (*Acanthopleura* and others) — great card, thin verbs ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiton))
- **Front:** radula with teeth coated in **magnetite**, 17 a row ✓ (bite on rock film; verb graze).
- **Head:** none; eyes are in the shell: **aesthetes**, some with **aragonite lenses**, thousands per animal ✓ (senses).
- **Back:** **eight shell plates** ✓; rolls into a ball when knocked off ✓ (defence; verb brace). **Sides:** the
  **girdle** with spicules or scales ✓. **Limbs:** the foot, clings like a limpet ✓.
- **Traits:** *home scar* (returns to the same spot after feeding ✓: a homing trait for the board); *roll up* ✓.
- **Card:** "It sees through eyes made of its own shell." ✓

### Atlantic horseshoe crab, *Limulus polyphemus* — card first ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_horseshoe_crab))
- **Front:** small **chelicerae** pass food to the mouth; no jaws; grinds food with leg bristles and a gizzard of
  sand ✓ (bite low, thrift high). **Head:** **nine eyes**, two compound eyes with about 1,000 ommatidia each ✓.
- **Limbs:** walking legs; males have clasping hooks ✓. **Back:** the **prosoma** carapace ✓ (defence). **Tail:** the
  **telson** steers and helps it flip back over ✓ (verb brace: a right-yourself rung).
- **Traits:** *blue blood* (haemocyanin, copper; its cells clot around bacteria ✓); *spring-tide spawning* (15,000–
  64,000 eggs in nests 15–20 cm deep on spring tides ✓).
- **Card:** "Its blood turns blue in air and is used to test medicines." ✓ / ~ (the LAL test is on PR 3's page).

### Bearded fireworm, *Hermodice carunculata* — fold into the ragworm line ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermodice_carunculata))
- Stinging white **bristles** on every segment flare and inject a neurotoxin ✓; a **caruncle** on the first four
  segments ✓; red gills along each side ✓; 60–150 segments, up to 30 cm ✓.
- **Use:** the real anchor for a ragworm-line *sides* part ("Fire bristles", defence + a sting) and the `venom`
  trait. Not a kit of its own.

### Flightless cormorant and solar sea slug — one trait each
- **Cormorant** ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flightless_cormorant)): wings a third of flying size; feathers
  not waterproof, it dries its wings after each dive; dives within 200 m of shore; nests of seaweed; about 1,500–
  1,700 birds ✓. A mutation of the *loss* kind (wings shrink, diving grows) or a story card, not a kit.
- **Solar sea slug** ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleptoplasty)): keeps chloroplasts up to ten months; the
  survival benefit is **unresolved** ✓. A *thrift* trait on a slug, carded honestly ("maybe").

## Part 2: new candidates

### Blue-ringed octopus, *Hapalochlaena* ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hapalochlaena))
- **Front:** **beak** that pierces crab shell and lets venom in ✓ (bite; verb crush, or a venom bite).
- **Sides:** **50–60 rings** that flash blue in under a third of a second; dark chromatophores under them expand
  within a second ✓. **New verb candidate: Flash** (a warning that stops a strike before it lands).
- **Limbs:** eight arms ~ (speed). **Head:** eyes ~. Size 12–20 cm ✓.
- **Traits:** *tetrodotoxin* (made by bacteria in its salivary gland ✓: the `venom` trait); *one clutch* (about 50
  eggs once, brooded under her arms for about six months without eating, then she dies ✓: a Life that ends in
  its lay).
- **Card:** "Bacteria make its venom, and no antivenom exists." ✓

### Portuguese man o' war, *Physalia physalis* ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_man_o%27_war))
- A **colony of seven kinds of zooids** ✓: the float, hunters (dactylozooids), feeders (gastrozooids), breeders
  (gonozooids). That is a kit already: each zooid is a part.
- **Back:** the **pneumatophore**, up to about 30 cm, a sail; gas includes up to 13% carbon monoxide it makes ✓.
  Rungs: *float* → *sail* → *dive* (it deflates to sink when attacked ✓).
- **Front/tail:** **tentacles** typically about 10 m, over 30 m stretched; stings kill fish ✓ (bite at range).
- **Traits:** *left- or right-sailed* (sails 45° off the wind, one hand each way, so at most half reach shore ✓: a
  look mutation with a board effect); *colony* (all zooids are clones ✓).
- **Card:** "It is not one animal but a colony, and the wind steers it." ✓

### Blue dragon, *Glaucus atlanticus* ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glaucus_atlanticus))
- Floats **upside down** on an air bubble in its stomach; blue above, silver below ✓ (countershading: defence).
- Eats the man o' war and **stores its stings in its own cerata** (kleptocnidy) ✓: a trait that takes a part
  from the food. About 3 cm, lives up to a year ✓.
- **Use:** the pair with the man o' war. *Idea:* eating a food with a defence grants that defence for the Life.
- **Card:** "It eats the man o' war and keeps its stings." ✓

### Sally Lightfoot crab, *Grapsus grapsus* ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grapsus_grapsus))
- About 8 cm carapace; **agile, leaps**, very fast reactions ✓ (verb dash). Eats algae, molluscs, crabs, carrion,
  eggs ✓ (a generalist: every gate).
- **Young are black and blend into black lava**; adults turn red, pink, yellow ✓: a colour that changes with life
  stage, made for a volcanic island board.
- Cleans ticks off **marine iguanas** (already on the roster) ✓: a cleaner trait that links two clades.
- **Fits:** the shore crab line (line 4) as a relative. **Card:** "Young ones are black to hide on lava." ✓

### Banded sea krait, *Laticauda* ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_krait))
- **Paddle tail** for swimming; **wide belly scales** kept from land snakes for crawling ✓.
- **Must come ashore** to digest and lay eggs ✓: the one land crosser that crosses both ways every meal.
- Hunts **moray and conger eels**, flushing them from reef crevices ✓; venom strong, bites rare ✓. Up to 1.5 m ✓.
- **Kit:** front fangs (venom), sides belly scales (land speed), tail paddle (sea speed): one body tuned for two
  realms. **Card:** "It hunts eels at sea and sleeps off its meal on land." ✓

### Fiddler crab, *Uca* ([Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uca))
- Males have one **much larger claw**; females two equal ✓. Waves it, slowly to the world and fast at a mate;
  neighbours wave in sync ✓. A lost big claw **regrows on the same side** at the next moult, weaker ✓.
- Sifts sediment and leaves **sand balls** by the burrow ✓ (verb graze); several burrows each; some build **sand
  pillars** ✓ (dig). Females release eggs on the falling tide ✓.
- **Use:** a mangrove crab where the board already has mangroves. Big claw is a *brood* part, not a *bite* part.

## Part 3: new body plans as kits
Each is a whole clade's kit. What it needs from the kit that `kit.ts` does not have today is flagged.

| Body plan | Example kit (6 places) | Signature mechanic | Needs from the kit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cephalopod** (cuttlefish, octopus, bobtail squid) | front arms/tentacles, head eyes, sides skin, back cuttlebone or none, tail siphon, limbs fins | hiding + jet + ink; short lives, one big lay | "miss" defence (hiding), an escape once a beat, a Life ending in its lay |
| **Drifting cnidarian colony** (man o' war) | back float/sail, front hunters, sides feeders, tail breeders; no head, no limbs | moved by wind, not by walking; stings at range | a mover that drifts; places renamed per plan; tentacle reach |
| **Sea star** (crown-of-thorns ~) | five arms as *limbs* (more arms as rungs), mouth under as front, spines as sides; no head, no tail | eats coral (10 m² a year ✓ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acanthaster)); stomach outside the body ~ | radial body: empty places (also urchins, PR 3) |
| **Air-breathing land arrival** (mudskipper, sea krait, Sally Lightfoot, coconut crab) | two parts per need: one for sea, one for land | crossing the shore line; the key adaptation opens a realm | a part that works in one realm only; the realm on the board |

**Lean (*proposal*):** the cephalopod plan first. It reuses six places as they are, has the most real parts, and the
three species in these docs give a ladder from small (bobtail squid) to big (cuttlefish). Its one ask, "miss" as a
defence, also serves the frogfish, the Sally Lightfoot and the blue dragon.

## New verbs the research suggests (budget 8, today 6)
Snap (pistol shrimp, ranged stun), Lure (frogfish, food comes to you), Flash (blue-ringed octopus, a warning),
Lunge (cuttlefish). Two fit the budget. **Lean:** Lure and Flash: each changes what a turn feels like, while Snap
and Lunge are crush and dash at range.
