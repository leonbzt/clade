# R1: What brings players back, waiting or filling gaps?

Researched 4 Nov 2026. Fetch budget ran out near the end (see Gaps).

## Summary
- Across the games checked, the pull evidence is for **named gaps the player can act on**, not for waiting. No source found that credits "waiting to see what changed" as a return reason.
- Pokédex-style grids work because blank entries are visible and curiosity-bearing (Tajiri), and because the gap is reachable by a known action.
- They turn into chores when the gap is only reachable by RNG repetition (OSRS drops, Melvor mastery) or by upkeep that is not the fun part (Mewgenics house, IdleOn dailies).
- Best-evidenced pattern for Clade: gaps that are **verbs** (play a new role, class or slugcat) pull; gaps that are **counts** (kill 3,784 times) become grind.
- Persistent-world games (Rain World) pull through the player's own runs, not through watching the world change.
- Evidence quality: mostly reviews and one dev article; no controlled data. Player quotes are single voices.
- Idle-genre return-reason research (Pecorella GDC) was not read; treat the waiting side as under-evidenced, not disproven.

## Gaps as pull

- **Pokédex (primary-ish).** Tajiri (1997 interview, translated): the Pokédex is "the result of trying to express the joy of collecting"; curiosity about blank entries drives players to fill them. He says some players try to fill the dex, others collect cute ones, and he wanted both. [Lavacut translation](https://lavacutcontent.com/?p=9758) (fan translation; I did not see the original).
- Version-split dex made the gap social: you needed the other version to complete the 150. [Herbert Lui, secondary](https://herbertlui.net/?p=5525)
- **Desktop Dungeons.** Gold from challenges builds a town; buildings unlock 16 classes and 11 races, each adding monsters with unique abilities. The reviewer credits unlocks for "a tremendous amount of replay value." Gaps here are roles to play. [Gaming Trend](https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/roguelike-dungeon-crawling-masterpiece-desktop-dungeons-review) (one review; 16/11 counts are the reviewer's).
- **Mewgenics.** Five classes at start; the rest unlock by first-clearing each area and returning home for a collar; later collars from late bosses. Persistent: house, furniture, lineages, NPC upgrades paid in retired cats. [Mewgenics wiki, fan](https://mewgenicswiki.org/articles/meta-progression-unlocks-guide)
- Pull in Mewgenics reviews is the run: combat as "an irresistible focal point" with constant discovery ([Game File](https://www.gamefile.news/p/mewgenics-review)); "come out of every run with a story" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/mewgenics-review)); "absolute blast", addicting ([Punished Backlog](https://punishedbacklog.com/mewgenics-review/)).
- **Path of Achra.** Runs carry no power, but new starting culture/class/religion options unlock as you play, and the reviewer notes they "occupy niches" rather than outclass old ones. Beating it unlocks Glory (difficulty and speed). Challenge completions reveal lore. [RIB16 review](https://destrierstudios.blogspot.com/2024/08/path-of-achra-review-rib16.html) (one reviewer; "highly addicting" is his word).
- **Rain World.** Persistent, unscripted creatures; the player sits mid food chain. Base game has 3 slugcats; Downpour adds 5, Watcher a 9th. Reviewers praise unscripted discovery; complaints are difficulty and karma gates. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_World) Aggregated Downpour reviews: new slugcats with distinct abilities are the most-cited pull, and each campaign has its own story and mechanics; Spearmaster is called repetitive and Artificer dull. [VaporLens summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/1933390/rain_world_downpour) (aggregator, not quotes).
- Reading for Clade: Rain World's world is alive, but players return to **play as something else in it**. That matches the playtest: "really wanted to play as a predator."

## Waiting as pull (weak evidence)

- IdleOn: retention is credited to "an addictive progression loop, with constant goals and numbers that keep growing", plus content volume, Discord and updates, not to watching change. [VaporLens summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/1476970/idle_on.md)
- Cogmind's developer designs achievements as aids to exploration and style ("hint at hidden content", "suggest play styles"); it is a no-meta-power roguelike where returning is for the next run. [Kyzrati, Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-and-building-a-robust-comprehensive-achievement-system)
- Counter-point I did not verify: idle design talks (Pecorella) say players check in for new unlocks and prestige. Not read; do not cite.
- Skipping time was fun for the Clade player ("already cool"), so change is a **reward for acting**, not a reason to open the game.

## When grids and logs become chores

- **OSRS collection log** (added 6 Dec 2018): 1,925 slots, 1,734 unique entries, ranks from 100 to 1,550 items. [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Collection_log) Poll 74 (2021) QoL changes passed 80.9% to 96.7% (total count at top, section colour on completion, search, kill counts). [Poll](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Poll:Old_School_Content_Poll_74:_Collection_Log_%26_Game_Improvements) Players want the grid to be easier to read and track, not removed.
- First player to green the boss log: about 22,000 hours played overall; one last drop took 3,784 kills (~1,000 h). Community debate on whether near-impossible slots (3rd Age, gilded) should be in the log at all; estimates of 10-60 years. [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/22000-hours-in-mmo-player-becomes-the-first-person-to-get-every-single-boss-drop-in-one-of-the-longest-grinds-ever) Chore marker: slots gated by pure RNG.
- **Melvor mastery:** players call 99 mastery on every item "pretty boring", click and wait, and advise stopping short of 95% on leftover skills. [Reddit via mirror](https://lr.sudovanilla.org/r/MelvorIdle/comments/1fjjj3n/how_maddening_is_the_mastery_grind/lnrzpln/?context=3) (one thread).
- **IdleOn:** daily and weekly tasks "repetitive", each new character repeats the same routine, "busywork"; but overall also praised. [VaporLens summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/1476970/idle_on.md) (aggregator; also covers pay-to-win complaints).
- **Mewgenics:** one reviewer: between-run cat management "is a chore", "feels like work", skipped days; another reviewer treats the same loop as core and fun. Complaints elsewhere: breeding as a "crapshoot", 2-hour runs, RNG. [Game File](https://www.gamefile.news/p/mewgenics-review), [Punished Backlog](https://punishedbacklog.com/mewgenics-review/) Opposing views, so it is taste, but the chore reading comes from **managing**, not from collecting.
- **Niche:** praised for genetics and adapting lineages to new islands; most frequent complaint is repetition and tedious micromanagement with weak long-term goals. [VaporLens summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/440650/niche_a_genetics_survival_game.md) Closest game to Clade's audience; I did not read primary Steam reviews.
- **Cogmind's rule:** "don't reward tedium"; achievements are single-run, none for "destroy 10 of each robot", hard ones hidden. [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-and-building-a-robust-comprehensive-achievement-system) The dev's claim that players ignore tutorials but read achievements is anecdotal.

## Rules that fit the evidence (my synthesis, not sourced as one claim)
1. A gap pulls if it is visible, named and reachable by a chosen action.
2. It turns to chore if reaching it needs repeating the same action for RNG, or if upkeep must happen before play.
3. Completion should not be gated on the long tail (OSRS debate). Let a grid be "done enough".
4. Roles that change how you play (classes, slugcats) outlast counts.

## Ideas for Clade
- **idea**: Make the garden's headline the empty niches: each open role/habitat shown as a named, playable gap with a reachable first step, so "I couldn't play it" disappears.
- **idea**: Give every trophic role a different Life verb (predator hunts, filter feeder anchors) so filling a role means a new way to play, not a higher number.
- **idea**: Keep grids to small named sets with a "complete enough" reward, no RNG-only slots and no upkeep gate before a Life (the Mewgenics house, IdleOn dailies lesson).

## Contradicts or tests big-picture.md
- §6 ranks "it changes while you're away" first for aliveness and §7 calls variants "the idle → active pull". The evidence here supports reordering: the pull is open niches and roles; change news is garnish. (Consistent with §0 and playtest 08.)
- §2/§3: "Nobody picks a role from a menu" works against the strongest pull seen (class/slugcat choice). Worth a toy test of letting the player pick a role to fill.

## Gaps and uncertainty
- WebFetch budget ran out before reading Pecorella's idle talk, Gamedeveloper's "games you mostly don't play", Quantic Foundry (completion motivation), Steam reviews of Mewgenics and Niche directly, and Rain World forum threads.
- Mewgenics evidence is reviews (about 3 weeks to a few months post-release, Feb 2026 onward), not Steam text. Steam is 90.7% positive of 49,661 reviews per an aggregator ([VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/686060/mewgenics/stats/details)).
- Tajiri's boys/girls remark is his, dated 1997; use only for "two collecting styles".
