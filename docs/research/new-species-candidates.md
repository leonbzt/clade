# New species that fit Clade (research, 17 Nov)

Candidates for the roster and for later lines, chosen for the theme (a young volcanic island's shallow sea, shore
and first land), a striking real ability, and being recognisable. All are movers (direction §1 3a). **Ideas, not
the plan:** none is added to the roster here; a pick gets one line in `ideas.md` or a W3 species batch.

**Grade:** ✓ = read on the cited page today; ~ = known biology, check before use.

## Summary
- **Worm relatives** (cheap to open next to the ragworm line, direction §5): the bloodworm (copper jaws), the palolo
  worm (a tail that swims off to spawn, harvested on Samoa) and the bearded fireworm (stinging bristles). These also
  give the ragworm kit real anchors for "Copper jaws", "venom" and "epitoky".
- **Snail relatives** for the periwinkle line: the chiton (eyes in its shell, magnetite teeth), the geography cone
  (a venom harpoon that kills fish) and the solar sea slug (keeps the chloroplasts it eats).
- **Showpiece abilities:** mantis shrimp (the fastest strike), pistol shrimp (a bubble hotter than the Sun's face),
  frogfish (a lure, a 6 ms gulp), bobtail squid (a light organ run by bacteria).
- **Land crossers** that fit "land by evolution": the mudskipper (a fish that walks and breathes through its skin),
  the coconut crab (already on the roster), the flightless cormorant (an island bird that lost flight).
- **Living fossil:** the horseshoe crab, 445 million years of the same body, blue blood.

## Further names, not read today (~)
- **Fiddler crab** (*Uca*): one giant claw for waving; mangrove mud; fits the mangroves already on the board.
- **Banded sea krait** (*Laticauda*): a sea snake that comes ashore to digest and lay eggs: another land crosser.
- **Archerfish** (*Toxotes*): spits water to knock insects off mangrove leaves.
- **Pufferfish** (white-spotted, *Torquigener albomaculosus*): a male draws a 2 m sand circle to court.
- **Decorator crab** (Majoidea): sticks weed and sponge on its shell; **boxer crab** (*Lybia*) holds anemones as gloves.
- **Lionfish** (*Pterois*): venom spines; **stonefish** (*Synanceia*): the most venomous fish, survives out of water.
- **Blue-ringed octopus** (*Hapalochlaena*): tetrodotoxin from bacteria; rings flash as a warning.
- **Crown-of-thorns starfish** (*Acanthaster*): eats coral, venomous spines, outbreaks on reefs.
- **Hermit crab** (*Coenobita*): shell-swap queues (vacancy chains) as a board event.
