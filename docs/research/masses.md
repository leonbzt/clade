# Rough masses for slot sizes (order of magnitude)

Web search gave few clean numbers. Confidence: S = from a cited source, E = my estimate (no source found; check before relying on it).
Plants are dry weight per m² at full cover. Animals are live weight per individual.

| Item | Value | Conf | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cyanobacterial crust | ~50 g/m² dry | E | Only hit: 165 g/m² WET hydrated cyanobacteria in a patent (low quality). Crust is mm thick. |
| Thalloid early land plant mat | ~50 g/m² | E | Thin-mat guess, no source. |
| Liverwort mat | ~100 g/m² | E | No source found. |
| Moss carpet | ~150 g/m² | S | Live boreal feather moss 47-230 g/m². Whole cushions 570-650 g/m². |
| Crust/foliose lichen cover | ~100 g/m² | S/E | Reindeer lichen, Norway: mostly <150 g/m² grazed, up to ~700 ungrazed. Crustose on rock is lower (~20-50, E). |
| Soil fungi (hyphae, topsoil) | ~20 g/m² dry | S | Ectomycorrhizal mycelium 110-800 kg/ha = 11-80 g/m². Method-dependent, ranges are wide. |
| Club moss layer | ~200 g/m² | E | No source found. |

| Springtail | ~0.1 mg | E | Confirmed as order of magnitude (small species ~0.03 mg, big ones ~0.3 mg). |

| Litter, thick | ~3000 g/m² | S | Conifer floors 1000-5000. Deciduous 500-1250. |
| Litter, thin | ~100 g/m² | E | About half a year of litterfall. Boreal litterfall is 210-274 g/m²/yr. |

## Use in Clade (proposal)
- Show plant slot sizes as round numbers (50 / 100 / 200 g/m²) and animal sizes as mg and g. Don't claim more precision than this.
- Pioneer layers (crust, thallus, liverwort) sit at 50-100 g/m². Moss, lichen, club moss and fern sit at 100-200. Litter is the biggest pool at 100-3000. That ordering matches the succession.
