# Progression in hand-curated idle/RPG games (IdleOn, Idle Obelisk Miner, Melvor, OSRS)

## Summary
- Common thread: a fixed, readable list of "next things" (skills, quests, tiers, checkpoints) that the player can see and choose between, with permanent account-level payoffs.
- Small capped numbers (OSRS/Melvor 1-99) stay meaningful because each level unlocks a named thing; hidden XP curves make the last levels slow.
- Parallel progress (IdleOn characters, Melvor skills) works when each lane feeds a shared account layer. It fails when the number of layers outgrows the player's attention.
- Evidence is thin for IdleOn overload and Idle Obelisk Miner (store text and one 2023 review only). Flagged below.
- Surtsey gull claim: broadly true, dates need softening (see end).

## OSRS
- Unlock gates: quest and skill requirements, no classes; progression is XP in individual skills. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)
- Achievement Diaries: 12 regions x 4 tiers (Easy to Elite), each with quest and skill requirements. Tasks go in any order, but tier rewards need earlier tiers. Rewards are permanent perks (teleports, better gathering). [Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Achievement_Diary), [guide, secondary](https://osrstoolkit.com/tools/achievement-diary/)
- Meta-goals: Quest point cape needs all quest points; the trimmed cape needs all diaries. Lumbridge Elite diary needs the untrimmed quest cape (a deliberate loop). [Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Quest_point_cape)
- Choice: no fixed class; the player sets goals. Updates are polled in-game (70% approval since 2022). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape)
- Numbers: level 92 is about half the XP of 99 (6,517,253 vs 13,034,431); a 200M XP cap per skill. So 1-99 is a display; the real curve is exponential. [Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Experience)
- Not found: Jagex's stated design reasons for diaries. Treat the above as mechanics only.

## Melvor Idle
- Mastery: each item/action has its own 1-99 mastery. Each skill also has a Mastery Pool with checkpoints at 10/25/50/95% that give bonuses only while the pool stays that full. Spending the pool on item mastery can drop you below a checkpoint, so it is a real trade-off. [Wiki](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Mastery), [Steam thread, player sentiment](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/discussions/0/3199241400250725473)
- Cross-skill feeding: some checkpoints give bonuses to other skills (e.g. Firemaking 95%: +5% Mastery XP in all skills, Melvor realm). [Wiki](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Firemaking) Melvor Idle 2 is described as having every skill serve the others and a level cap of 120. [Deku Deals listing](https://www.dekudeals.com/items/melvor-idle-2)
- Offline: one forum post says offline progress is simulated exactly, mastery unlocks included (player claim, not verified). [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1267910/discussions/0/3199241400250725473)
- Numbers: 1-99 plus mastery 1-99 per item; small numbers, many of them.

## Legends of IdleOn
- Parallel characters: every character has its own class/skills, and the account shares layers on top. Family Bonuses use your highest-level character per class; The Tome bonuses use total account level across all characters; Legend Talent Points come from class levels summed over the account. [Family bonuses](https://www.digitaltq.com/wiki/idleon/family-bonuses), [Tome](https://www.digitaltq.com/wiki/idleon/the-tome), [Legend Talents](https://www.digitaltq.com/wiki/idleon/legend-talents) (DigitalTQ, a fan wiki)
- Gates by world: each character must visit World 2 town and click the cauldron before it gains Alchemy XP; others stay at level 0 in World 2 skills. A Steam user got stuck on exactly this. [Fan wiki](https://idleon.wiki/wiki/Sigil), [Steam, player report](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1476970/discussions/0/3055114706801476382)
- Alchemy: four tabs, cauldrons with at most two characters each by default, and one wiki warns that neglecting it costs account-wide bonuses. [Guide, commercial](https://gameslikefinder.com/article/idleon-alchemy-guide/)
- Overload: I found no review text on this. The evidence is indirect: the existence of a Tome, Trophies and weekly votes (Multi Meritocracy) shows many stacked account layers needing upkeep. [Multi Meritocracy](https://www.digitaltq.com/wiki/idleon/multi-meritocracy-guide) The "feels overwhelming" claim is unverified here; check Steam reviews before relying on it.
- Numbers: inflating, with account-wide multipliers on top of per-character levels.

## Idle Obelisk Miner (low confidence)
- Sources are store listings and one 2023 TapTap review only. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/app/id6448125670), [TapTap review](https://www.taptap.io/post/4550050)
- Layers: Obelisk (build stats to beat it, which unlocks content), many upgrade trees locked behind milestones, bombs via the workshop, and later systems (Stargazing, Archaeology, Fishing). Prestige resets upgrades; relics stay; artifacts cost prestige points. v2.0 added over 1300 completion points. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/app/id6448125670), [TapTap](https://www.taptap.io/post/4550050)
- Praise (one reviewer): active and idle play both work; positive overall. Criticism (same reviewer, 2023): early prestige is weak, the early game is not idle until drones are upgraded, no cloud saves. [TapTap](https://www.taptap.io/post/4550050)
- Not found: pacing data, Steam sentiment, numbers handling.

## Tierzoo (framing)
- YouTube channel (2017, Patrick "Patch" Lacey) that treats animals as game "builds" in an MMO called "Outside", with tier lists, "meta" and "spec" vocabulary and RuneScape references. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TierZoo), [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/people/tierzoo) Good proof that the Clade audience already thinks "species = build".

## Surtsey gull check
- Verdict: supported, with a softened date. A dense gull colony formed from 1985 (some sources say 1986) and grew to several hundred breeding pairs within a few years. Gulls moved about 45-50 kg N/ha/yr into the colony against 1-2 kg from the atmosphere elsewhere. Colony areas have much higher cover, richness, plant biomass and soil carbon. Bird dispersal became the main plant route after 1985; wind-dispersed species established after 1990. [Magnússon et al. 2014, Biogeosciences](https://www.biogeosciences.net/11/5521/2014/), [Surtsey Research Society](https://english.surtsey.is/adflutningur-lifvera/)
- Caveats: write "from the mid-1980s". Later the oldest colony areas became dominated by a few rhizomatous grasses with lower richness, and there is a net species loss since 2007 (same paper). Good "succession is not monotone" material.

## Use in Clade (all **proposal**)
- **Visible next steps:** show a diary-style grid (region/guild x 4 tiers, tasks in any order, tier rewards need earlier tiers) as the tree-of-life map. Locked nodes show their requirement text, as OSRS quest requirements do.
- **Lineage slots:** copy IdleOn's shared layer. Each slot has its own minigame; a small account-wide "gene pool" bonus comes from the best species per guild (Family Bonus rule), so a second same-guild slot is not a pure duplicate. Releasing = slot prestige that banks a permanent gene pool entry (like Melvor checkpoints that stay).
- **Overload guard:** cap active layers (2-3 slots at first) and make every new layer idle-able before the next opens; this answers IdleOn's upkeep problem (indirect evidence only). Also avoid IdleOn's "visit to activate" gate trap: no hidden activation step.
- **Key innovations:** make them the quest-like gates (requirement list, then a named reward), with a meta-goal such as "complete all clades of a guild" (quest cape idea).
- **Niche fit tiers:** use Melvor's pool/checkpoint idea. Fit fills a bar; thresholds at 10/25/50/95% give named bonuses, and over-spending resources elsewhere can drop you under a threshold (a choice without lockout).
- **Bundles:** Stardew-style bundles as the OSRS diary equivalent: one region, a few species/guilds, a reward that boosts all slots.
- **Relationships:** reuse cross-skill feeding: a completed relationship (pollinator and flower, grazer and grass) gives a small bonus to the partner's checkpoint.
- **Numbers:** keep visible levels small and named (1-99-like fit tiers), put exponentials behind the scenes, and show the "92 is half of 99" feel for late tiers. Use Surtsey-style succession (gulls enabling plants) as a flagship relationship.
