# Slice 12: open your clutch

**Milestone:** M1, step 5 (ROADMAP; built after 13, as plan.md allows)
**Question:** does the clutch pull you into the next Life?
**Builds on:** decisions.md, 5 Nov: "there should also be some kind of lootbox feeling maybe"; "b) open your clutch, c later";
after a Life, eggs hatch as cards, rare parts, you pick the form. tastes.md: rare mutations "felt really compelled to go on";
rarity in game-like tiers; rare drops and milestones you worked for.

## In
- **The clutch:** after a Life with eggs, 2–5 eggs (2 + one per 8 eggs laid) lie face down. Tap one to hatch it: a
  hatchling card with a form = one part, at a rarity: *common* (a part of your body plan), *rare* (one of this habitat's
  rares), *epic* (a rare from another habitat). Better odds at a higher tier.
- **Pick the form:** keep one card. Your line in that niche carries its part from then on: every later Life there starts
  with it on the body, beside your verbs. It replaces the form you had there.
- The habitat card shows each of your lines' forms. Then the eggs are released as before.

## Out
- Garden variants as chests (later, decided "c later"); forms that spread between habitats; breeding.
