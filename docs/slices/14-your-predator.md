# Slice 14: your predator hunts you, and the trilobite

**Milestone:** M1, step 7 (ROADMAP)
**Question:** does your own build as the hunter make a story?
**Builds on:** decisions.md, 5 Nov: your best predator body in a habitat becomes that board's hunter once it ranks top; "maybe
some special rules or mechanics for the predator would also be cool"; the trilobite, "maybe evolve from arthropods";
plan.md proposal: unlocked by a first A tier as an arthropod.

## In
- **Your hunter:** when your swimmer line holds the top score in a habitat's hunter row, the grazers' and burrowers' boards
  there get your swimmers as the hunter: your colours, your name, and your rules turned on you:
  - it *lurks*: a turn still, then it swims twice as far (your ambush);
  - it gains a step after each hit on you (your frenzy);
  - your line's form changes it: compound eyes see you through mud; swimming flaps add a step; arm spines tear a part
    even through a guard.
  The habitat card and the start card say so; news says when it happens.
- **The trilobite** (a body plan): unlocked by your first A tier as an arthropod (news and a card). Grazer niches then offer
  it beside the arthropod. It runs 2 tiles in a line, but mud doesn't stop it, it costs double; it rolls up when it stays put
  (strikes glance off); a third pair of side slots; drawn with a broad head shield, three lobes and a tail shield.
