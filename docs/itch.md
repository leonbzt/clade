# The itch page (draft for slice 15; yours to change)

**Title:** Clade
**Short line:** Live one creature's season at a time; its eggs grow a living Cambrian sea.

**Text:**
A young volcanic island, 500 million years ago. Around it, three habitats: sand shallows, mud flats, volcanic rock.
Each has niches (grazer, burrower, hunter) and a tier list of the species that hold them.

Pick an open niche and live one Life there: 20 turns as a worm, an arthropod or a swimming hunter. Walk, tunnel, graze,
dig, hunt. Grow up, moult new parts, and lay eggs before the season ends or something eats you.

Your eggs hatch: keep the best form for your line. They join the garden, which keeps living while you're away, and
the board you play next is what the garden became. Take the hunter row and your own hunters start chasing your grazers.

- Plays in the browser, on a phone too. Saves in your browser (Menu → copy your save).
- Share a species code on the Discord; paste a friend's and their species turns up in your garden, to beat.
- A hobby project, early. The animals are real; the sea is a composite of the Cambrian.

**Upload:** `npm run build:game`, then upload `game/dist/clade.html` as an HTML game ("This file will be played in the
browser"), viewport 420 × 860 or "click to launch in fullscreen" for phones.
