Sea board toy

Each cell is a job in a habitat: graze, dig or hunt. Tap a cell, then live a Life there. Gold is yours, grey is a wild cousin, blue is open. A red bar on an open cell is a wild cousin on its way. F means it can't hold and is dying out.

Chart, : grams of your food-eaters (gold), wild ones (grey) and hunters (red), last 240 generations.
