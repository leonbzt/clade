Body grammar toy

One grammar, seven lines. Per line: three builds (animals) or three ages (modular). Per cell: the portrait (the board sprite × 3), then the board sprite and its silhouette (× 2), then the map icon, its silhouette (× 2) and the icon at true size.

Size is a gene: 12, 16, 20, 24 px

The head grows slower than the body (head share ∝ length−0.25), so a big animal reads as big even at the same zoom.
