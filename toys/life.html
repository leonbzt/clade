Rules

Each turn: move (click a marked tile), lay eggs, or stay and turn. Then you pay upkeep, the rival grazes, and the predator acts. Landing on food eats it unless it's locked. Moulting costs food, adds a segment and gives you a part; it doesn't use your turn.

A lay costs 4 food and gives (1 + pouches) × (1 + size ÷ 2, rounded down) eggs. Out of food: your body absorbs its newest part, then you lose health. Out of oxygen: you lose health.

The predator hunts you and marks the tiles it will strike next. A strike from your side tears off a part on that side, or costs 1 health if the side is bare. A guard on that side blocks it. You face the way you last moved, so how you arrive decides which side it hits.

Eggs you laid count even if you die. A rare part only joins your lineage if you lay an egg after getting it.
