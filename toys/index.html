Clade: throwaway toys

Play each one for about 10 minutes. Which one do you keep coming back to?

The living garden (new)

Life v2 inside a garden that runs by itself: residents, tier lists, news, variants. Your eggs change the place, and the place writes your next board. Does coming back pull you into another Life?

A Life, v2

Pick a habitat in the garden: reef, mud flats or sulfur basin, each with its own map, threats, rare parts and tier scale. Grow up from a hatchling to a finished body, lay from size 3, and worms tunnel through mud.

A Life, v1

One creature on a Cambrian seafloor, 20 turns. Pick a body plan and starter parts, move, eat, moult to grow, and lay eggs before the season ends. A predator you can see coming, locked food, one rare mutation.

M1: A. Grow by turns (slice 04)

One plant on a side-view plot, 12 turns. Leaves make energy by their light, roots find hidden pockets; spend it on stem, leaf, root and flower cells. Shade, a drought, natives that drink. Three plots.

M1: C. Body builder (slice 04)

Draft 10 organ pieces onto a body grid. Keep energy, water and nutrient balanced; the 6 stats are scored by a real niche's weights; one encounter at the end.

Pixel fonts

Eleven free pixel fonts in the run's UI lines: size slider, drop shadow on or off.

M1: The look (slice 02)

One scene in two looks: 2D low-poly or simple 3D. Saplings grow, roots hunt hidden soil, the sun casts shadows; the island and a species card in the references' style. Toggle look, width, palette, shadows.

M0: Grow a line

The v2 toy (slice 00): pick a habitat and a target; genes grow a side-view plant over 5 seasons; wake buds, cut branches, dig through hidden soil; a brood of 3 seeds on different spots, pick the heir.

1. Patch

Fixed rules, spatial verbs: steer the spread, tap dew, catch spores, ride out dry spells.

2. Specimen

Grow one plant: shoots reach for light, roots for water. Shoo grazers.

3. Rules

Don't touch the map. Pick genes that rewrite the colony's rules, then watch the island.

4. Colony

Patch's growth, with real choices: limited spore drops, hidden ground with hint numbers, lichen to surround, a gene draft with combos, and slide to collect.
