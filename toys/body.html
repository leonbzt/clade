Rules

10 picks: each time pick one of three organ pieces and place it on the body (or skip). Shoot pieces touch a stem or wood; roots and tubers touch a root. Each piece makes or costs energy, water and nutrient each season: keep all three at 0 or more, or the body wilts (−15% score per unit short). The body's 6 stats are scored by the niche's real weights (a negative weight rewards a low stat). A body needs a flower to make seeds. When the picks are done, the encounter hits and the final tier is taken after it.
