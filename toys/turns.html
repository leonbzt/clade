Rules

Each turn your leaves make energy by their light (2 in open sun; each leaf or native cell between a leaf and the sun takes 1). Roots draw water (2) and nutrient (1) from pockets they sit on. Rain adds water. Every leaf drinks 1 water a turn: for each water short, your darkest leaf wilts. Roots reveal the soil next to them; blue specks count hidden damp pockets in the 8 cells around, orange specks rich ones. Natives drink from pockets their roots touch. One drought turn: no rain, roots draw 1, leaves drink 2. After 12 turns: 10 points per flower plus 1 per part (grams).
